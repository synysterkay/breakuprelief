## Should I Text My Ex? The Honest Answer

After a breakup, the question "Should I text my ex?" can feel like an itch you can't scratch. You miss them, you're lonely, or you just want answers. But before you hit send, let's take a breath and think this through.

### 1. Why Do You Want to Text?

Be brutally honest with yourself. Common reasons include:
- **Loneliness** – You miss the companionship.
- **Hope** – You believe they've changed or will come back.
- **Anger** – You want closure or to hurt them back.
- **Guilt** – You feel responsible for the breakup.

Most of these reasons are driven by emotion, not logic. If you're in pain, texting rarely brings relief.

### 2. What Are the Risks?

- **Slowing your healing** – Every text reopens the wound.
- **Misinterpretation** – They may read it differently than you intend.
- **Power imbalance** – You may appear desperate or needy.
- **No response** – That silence can hurt more than the breakup.

### 3. The 30-Day No Contact Rule

Experts often recommend at least 30 days of no contact. This isn't a game – it's a chance to:
- Regain emotional stability
- See the relationship clearly
- Build self-worth outside of them

### 4. Questions to Ask Before Texting

- **Is this about me or them?** If it's for your own relief, reconsider.
- **What's the best-case outcome?** Will it truly make you happy?
- **What's the worst-case?** Can you handle that?
- **Have I given myself enough time?** If it's been less than a month, wait.

### 5. Healthier Alternatives

- **Write an unsent letter** – Get it out of your system.
- **Talk to a friend** – They can offer perspective.
- **Use AI support** – Fresh Start acts as a private, non-judgmental coach.
- **Journal** – Track your feelings day by day.

> ⚠️ **Safety Note:** If you're experiencing depression, panic attacks, thoughts of self-harm, or are in an abusive situation, please reach out to a professional or crisis line immediately. This article is not a substitute for therapy.

### 6. When It Might Be Okay

There are rare exceptions:
- **Shared responsibilities** – Kids, pets, or joint finances.
- **Genuine apology** – If you hurt them and need to make amends.
- **Clarity** – If they left belongings and you need to return them.

Keep it short, neutral, and don't expect a reply.
