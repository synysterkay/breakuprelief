## Should I Text My Ex After a Breakup?

It's the question that echoes in your mind at 2 a.m. Your phone feels like a magnet, your fingers itch to type, and your heart races with a mix of hope and fear. But before you hit send, take a breath. This moment is about more than a text—it's about your healing.

### The Urge to Text: Why It's So Strong

Breakups create a vacuum. Your brain, used to the comfort of your ex's presence, screams for that familiar dopamine hit. Texting feels like a quick fix, but it's often a detour on the road to recovery.

**Common reasons you want to text:**
- Missing the connection
- Wanting answers or closure
- Hoping to rekindle the relationship
- Loneliness or boredom
- Seeing something that reminds you of them

### The No-Contact Rule: Your Best Friend Right Now

No contact isn't punishment—it's a gift to yourself. It gives your brain time to rewire, your heart space to grieve, and your identity a chance to stand on its own.

**Benefits of no contact:**
- Reduces anxiety and obsession
- Helps you regain clarity
- Builds self-worth and independence
- Prevents impulsive decisions you might regret

**Tip:** Block or mute your ex temporarily. Out of sight, out of mind really works.

### When Texting Might Be Okay (And When It's Not)

**Probably not a good idea if:**
- You're angry, sad, or drunk
- You're hoping to change their mind
- You haven't given yourself at least 30 days of space
- The relationship was toxic or abusive

**Possibly okay if:**
- You need to exchange belongings (keep it brief and neutral)
- You share children or pets (stick to logistics only)
- You've both healed and want a genuine friendship (rare, and usually after months)

**Safety note:** If you're experiencing depression, panic attacks, self-harm thoughts, or are in an abusive situation, please reach out to a crisis line or therapist immediately. No text can replace professional support.

### What to Do Instead of Texting

1. **Write it out** – Open a note app and pour your heart out. Don't send it. Just release the thoughts.
2. **Call a friend** – Let someone who cares about you remind you why you're amazing.
3. **Move your body** – A walk, yoga, or even dancing in your room shifts your energy.
