## Should I Text My Ex After a Breakup? 10 Questions to Ask Yourself First

It's 2 AM. Your thumb hovers over the send button. The urge to text your ex feels overwhelming. You've re-read old messages, imagined what you'd say, and convinced yourself that *this time* it'll be different.

Before you hit send, take a deep breath. Here's a practical guide to help you decide—and healthier ways to cope with the impulse.

### Why Do You Want to Text Them?

Be brutally honest. Are you looking for:
- **Closure**? (Spoiler: closure comes from within, not from them.)
- **Revenge**? (Will you really feel better?)
- **Loneliness**? (Temporary relief, longer pain.)
- **Hope of getting back together**? (If it was meant to be, time apart won't ruin it.)

### 10 Questions to Ask Yourself

1. **Am I in a calm emotional state?**
   If you're angry, sad, or drunk, wait 24 hours.

2. **What do I hope to achieve?**
   Be specific. Is it realistic?

3. **Have they asked me not to contact them?**
   Respect their boundary. No contact means no contact.

4. **Will this message help me heal?**
   Or will it reopen the wound?

5. **Am I prepared for any response—including silence?**
   Silence hurts more than a rejection.

6. **Is this about my ego?**
   If you want them to miss you, that's a red flag.

7. **Would I advise a friend to send this?**
   Listen to your own wisdom.

8. **Have I tried other coping strategies first?**
   Journaling, exercising, talking to a friend, or using the [Fresh Start app](https://apps.apple.com/app/fresh-start-breakup-therapy-ai/id6749954260) for private AI support?

9. **Am I using this as a distraction from my own growth?**
   Healing requires facing yourself, not reaching out.

10. **What's the best-case scenario? Worst-case?**
    Usually the worst is more likely.

### The 24-Hour Rule

Write your message in a notes app. Set a timer for 24 hours. When the time's up, re-read it. Most likely, you'll delete it. If you still want to send it, ask a trusted friend for their opinion.

- **Write an unsent letter**: Pour out everything, then burn it or save it.

- **Move your body**: A walk, run, or yoga session can shift your emotional state.
- **Call a friend**: Real human connection beats a risky text.

### When Texting Might Be Okay (Rare Cases)

1. **Returning essential items** (keys, documents) – Keep it brief and neutral.
2. **Emergency or legal matters** – Only if absolutely necessary.
3. **You're co-parenting** – Stick to logistics, no emotions.

### Safety Note

If you're experiencing thoughts of self-harm, severe depression, or panic, please reach out to a crisis line in your country immediately. While Fresh Start can offer supportive conversation, it is not a substitute for professional mental health care. You deserve real help—please seek it.

### Final Thought

Every time you resist the urge to text, you're choosing yourself. That's not selfish; it's survival. Healing is a quiet, private journey. You don't need to prove anything to your ex. You just need to prove to yourself that you can get through this.

And you will.
