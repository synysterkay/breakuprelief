## Should I Text My Ex After a Breakup?

It's one of the hardest questions after a breakup: *Should I text my ex?* Your heart might scream "yes," but your healing brain knows better. Let's explore why texting your ex is usually a bad idea — and what to do instead.

### The Urge to Text Is Normal

Breakups trigger withdrawal. Your brain craves the familiar comfort of your ex. But acting on that urge often leads to more pain. Before you type that message, ask yourself:

- What do I hope to achieve?
- Will this help me heal or set me back?
- Am I acting from loneliness or genuine need?

### Why No Contact Works

The no-contact rule gives you space to grieve, rebuild identity, and gain clarity. It's not punishment — it's self-care. During this time:

- Process emotions without interruptions
- Rediscover your own hobbies and friends
- Break the addiction to your ex

### What If You're Feeling Desperate?

If you're struggling with intense anxiety, depression, or thoughts of self-harm, **please reach out to a crisis line** (e.g., 988 in the US, 116 123 in the UK). You matter, and you don't have to go through this alone.

### The Only Time to Text Your Ex

There are rare exceptions — like logistics about shared property or pets. But even then, keep it brief and neutral. No emotional dumping, no "I miss you." Save that for your journal or Fresh Start.

### What to Do Instead of Texting

Feeling the urge? Try these:

1. **Write an unsent letter** — get it all out, then delete or burn it.
2. **Talk to Fresh Start** — it's like having a wise friend who never judges.
3. **Call a real friend** — connection reduces cravings.
4. **Go for a walk** — movement shifts your state.
