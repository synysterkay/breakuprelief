Breakup pain does not keep office hours. The hardest moment may happen at 1:37 a.m., after a dream, after seeing a photo, or right before you almost text your ex.

That is where an AI breakup coach can help.

Fresh Start is available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/fresh-start-breakup-therapy-ai/id6749954260) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.breakup.therapy.therapyforabreakup.therapistforbreakups).

## What AI can do well

AI support can help you slow down and reflect:

- "I want to text them. Help me pause."
- "I feel rejected. What do I do tonight?"
- "Help me write what I feel without sending it."
- "What is a healthier next step?"

It can also help you notice patterns. Are you seeking closure, reassurance, revenge, comfort, or proof that you mattered?

## What AI should not do

AI should not diagnose you, replace therapy, handle emergencies, or tell you to ignore real danger.

If you are at risk of harming yourself, being harmed, or harming someone else, contact emergency services, a crisis line, or a licensed professional immediately.

## Why private reflection matters

After a breakup, people often need to say the same messy thing several times before it becomes clear. Friends can help, but you may worry about exhausting them.

An AI breakup coach gives you a place to unload the first draft of your feelings, then turn it into something more grounded.

## The best use: pause before acting

Use AI before:

- Texting your ex.
- Checking their social media.
- Reopening an argument.
- Making a permanent decision from a temporary spike.

The goal is not to let AI run your life. The goal is to create enough space for your wiser self to come back online.
