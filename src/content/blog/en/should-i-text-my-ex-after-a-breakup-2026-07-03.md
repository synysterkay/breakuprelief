## Should I Text My Ex After a Breakup?

It's 3 AM. Your thumb hovers over their name. The urge to type "Hey..." feels unbearable. You're not alone – nearly everyone asks, *should I text my ex?* The short answer: almost always no. But let's unpack why.

### Why Reaching Out Usually Backfires

Texting an ex often comes from pain, loneliness, or a desire for closure. But here's the hard truth:

- **You're seeking comfort from the person who caused the pain.** That rarely works.
- **It resets your healing clock.** Every text reignites hope and anxiety.
- **You may get a reply that hurts more.** Silence, coldness, or breadcrumbing can set you back weeks.

### When Might It Be Okay?

There are rare exceptions:

- **Shared responsibilities** (kids, pets, lease) – keep messages purely logistical.
- **Returning important belongings** – brief and neutral.
- **Apologizing for a genuine wrong** – only if you've done real self-work and expect nothing back.

Even then, ask yourself: *Is this text truly necessary, or am I looking for relief?*

### The No Contact Rule: Your Best Bet

No contact isn't punishment – it's medicine. By cutting off communication, you:

- Give your brain's attachment system time to rebalance.
- Break the addiction of checking your phone.
- Rebuild your identity outside the relationship.

**Try this:** Commit to 30 days of no contact. Use that time to journal, exercise, and lean on friends. If you still feel the urge, use a private tool like **Fresh Start** – an AI breakup support app that lets you vent, get perspective, and practice responses without real-world consequences.

> **Safety Note:** If you're experiencing depression, panic attacks, self-harm thoughts, or are in an abusive relationship, please reach out to a crisis hotline (like 988 in the US) or a therapist. This article is not a substitute for professional help.

### What to Do Instead of Texting

1. **Write a letter you never send.** Get it all out – anger, sadness, hope – then delete or burn it.
2. **Call a friend.** Let someone else talk you off the ledge.
3. **Use Fresh Start.** The app offers private, judgment-free AI conversations to help you process your emotions anytime.
4. **Distract yourself.** Watch a show, go for a walk, cook a meal. The urge usually passes in 20 minutes.

### The Bottom Line

Texting your ex rarely brings the relief you're hoping for. It often reopens wounds and delays healing. Instead, invest that energy in yourself. You deserve a future where you don't need to look back.
