## Should I Text My Ex After a Breakup? 5 Questions to Ask Yourself First

Breakups are hard. Your phone is right there, and the urge to send a message—just a simple "Hey"—can feel overwhelming. But before you type, pause. Texting your ex too soon can undo your healing and reopen wounds.

Here are five questions to ask yourself before hitting send.

### 1. What is my real motivation?
Are you lonely? Do you want them back? Or are you hoping for closure? Be honest. If you're seeking validation or relief from pain, texting will likely make things worse. Instead, write down what you want to say in a private journal or use **Fresh Start**, a private AI breakup support app, to talk it out without judgment.

### 2. How long has it been since the breakup?
The first few weeks are the most fragile. Experts often recommend a **30-day no-contact rule** to give yourself space to heal. If it's only been a few days or weeks, wait. Time helps you see things more clearly.

### 3. What do I hope will happen?
Imagine the best-case scenario: they reply warmly. Then what? If you're hoping for reconciliation, ask yourself if the relationship was truly healthy. If it was toxic or abusive, reaching out can put you at risk. **Safety note:** If you are in an abusive relationship, please contact a domestic violence hotline or trusted professional before any contact.

### 4. Am I ready for any response?
They might not reply. They might be cold or angry. Could you handle that without spiraling? If you're still emotionally raw, protect yourself by waiting until you're more stable.

### 5. Is there a healthier way to cope?
Instead of texting, try these alternatives:
- Call a friend
- Go for a walk
- Write a letter and burn it
- Use the **Fresh Start** app for private AI conversations

### When to seek professional help
If you're struggling with depression, anxiety, panic attacks, or thoughts of self-harm, please reach out to a therapist or crisis line. Fresh Start is a supportive tool, but it is not a substitute for professional mental health care.

### Final thought
You don't have to text your ex to move on. Healing comes from within, not from their reply. Give yourself the gift of time and self-compassion.
