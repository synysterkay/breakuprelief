## Should I Text My Ex After a Breakup?

It's 3 AM, your heart aches, and your thumb hovers over their name. **Should you text your ex?** The short answer: probably not right now. But let's explore why—and what to do instead.

### The Emotional Trap

After a breakup, your brain is wired to seek comfort from the person who once provided it. That urge to text is natural, but it often leads to:
- **Regret**: Saying things you can't take back.
- **False hope**: Misinterpreting a reply as a sign to get back together.
- **Pain**: Reopening wounds before they've healed.

### When It Might Be Okay

There are rare exceptions, like:
- Returning important belongings.
- Discussing logistics (e.g., shared lease).
- Offering a genuine, heartfelt apology (without expecting reconciliation).

Even then, keep it brief and neutral. If you're hoping for a specific response, pause.

### The No Contact Rule

Many therapists suggest a **30-60 day no contact period**. This isn't a game—it's a chance to:
- Reconnect with yourself.
- Break the emotional dependency.
- Gain perspective on the relationship.

1. **Journaling** your feelings.
