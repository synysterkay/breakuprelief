## Should I Text My Ex After a Breakup?

Breaking up is hard. In the days and weeks that follow, your mind may replay memories, and the question "Should I text my ex?" can feel overwhelming. Before you type that message, take a moment to consider what's driving the urge and what the likely outcome will be.

### Why You Want to Text

Common reasons include:
- **Loneliness** – You miss the companionship.
- **Closure** – You feel you didn't get a proper ending.
- **Hope** – You think one message could rekindle things.
- **Habit** – You're used to sharing everything with them.

None of these are bad, but acting on impulse can delay your healing.

### The Risks of Texting Too Soon

- **Reopening wounds** – A reply (or silence) can trigger fresh pain.
- **Mixed signals** – You may end up more confused.
- **Losing progress** – Every day of no contact helps you move forward.
- **Reinforcing unhealthy patterns** – If the relationship was toxic, contact can pull you back in.

### What to Do Instead

1. **Wait 24 hours** – Give yourself a cooling-off period.
2. **Write but don't send** – Get your feelings out in a journal or note.
3. **Talk to someone** – A friend, therapist, or AI support like [Fresh Start](https://apps.apple.com/app/fresh-start-breakup-therapy-ai/id6749954260) (iOS) or [Fresh Start](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.breakup.therapy.therapyforabreakup.therapistforbreakups) (Android) can provide a safe space to process.
4. **Focus on self-care** – Exercise, hobbies, and rest rebuild your strength.

### When Texting Might Be Okay

There are rare situations where contact is reasonable:
- Returning essential belongings.
- Discussing shared responsibilities (e.g., pets, leases).
- After significant time (months or years) and both have moved on.

Even then, keep it brief and neutral.

### The No Contact Rule

A period of no contact (typically 30–60 days) gives you space to heal, regain perspective, and break the emotional dependency. It's not about punishment—it's about self-respect.

### Final Thoughts

The urge to text your ex is normal, but it doesn't mean you should. Ask yourself: "Will this help me heal or set me back?" Choose the path that honors your future self.
